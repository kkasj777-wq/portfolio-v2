# Design - Wang Chenxin / Author-Director Visual Monograph V7

This file locks the visual system for the 34-page PDF. It is a film catalogue, not a slide deck. Page variety comes from editorial structure and image rhythm, not from swapping coloured backgrounds.

## Genre

Editorial - cinematic archival monograph.

## Macrostructure family

- Primary: Photographic. A strong still can occupy an entire page or spread; captions remain small and factual.
- Secondary: Long Document. Director notes and screenplay pages read as printed matter, without cards.
- Archive pages: Catalogue only when the content is genuinely inventory-like, such as seven episodes, a storyboard contact sheet, or asset continuity evidence.

## Theme

- Paper: warm mineral white, never pure white.
- Ink: blue-black, never pure black.
- Silver: cool grey for captions and rules.
- Signal: one restrained red used only for editorial marks and verified production evidence.
- Project colour is allowed only when it comes from the work itself. Maimai may carry the book's only concentrated colour burst.

## Typography

- Display: Source Han Serif SC Heavy, roman, for project titles and rare declarative lines.
- Body: Noto Sans SC, regular, for Chinese reading text.
- Outlier: Inter SemiBold, used only for nameplate, page numbers and production data.
- No italic headings. No decorative all-caps labels on every page.

## Page rhythm

- The spread, not the single page, is the core unit.
- Alternate full-bleed stills, quiet paper pages, archive evidence, black-light pages and restrained contact sheets.
- At least eight pages are image-led or full-bleed.
- No fixed title position. No repeated header/footer template.
- Most pages carry only a folio number; section names appear only at genuine transitions.

## Image rules

- Use clean frames without subtitles, watermarks or short-video UI as primary imagery.
- Do not repeat the same still. If an image must recur, change its function and crop.
- Storyboards, script excerpts and asset sheets are evidence, not decoration.
- No invented stock or generated ornaments.

## Copy rules

- Preserve only verified facts already present in the project source.
- Director notes must describe a decision, consequence or method.
- Reduce explanation. A still should carry the emotion; a caption should identify the decision.
- No invented awards, metrics, platforms, links or QR codes.

## Banned structures

- Rounded cards, dashboards, equal feature grids and nested boxes.
- A repeated eyebrow above every page title.
- Decorative timelines, flowcharts or SmartArt.
- A single quote centred on an otherwise empty page unless the image/light composition materially earns it.
- Chapter-wide colour fills unrelated to the film stills.
